    Notes for your accountant

      What to prepare

      Safety buffer

      Bowl portions for the week

      Curries, bases and extras to have ready, worked out from expected bowl sales and how often each choice is picked.

      How the numbers are worked out

      • Expected is a weighted average of the last 4 full weeks. The newest week counts most (40%, 30%, 20%, 10%).
      • Prep adds the safety buffer and rounds up. Use +20% for items that sell out, or none for items that go to waste.
      • Daily numbers split the week by how that item usually sells on each day.
      • Items not sold in the last 2 weeks are left out. Items marked new have only a week or two of history.
      • The plan follows the location filter at the top. Holidays and campus events aren't in the data, so adjust for those.

        Item names combined

        The university file sometimes names the same item differently. These are counted as one item everywhere.

          Adding a new week

          • Use the weekly sales report the university sends (CSV or Excel, starting with columns Order Date, Time Interval, Campus, Venue).
          • Press Add weekly file and pick the file. Its days replace any days already loaded, so re-uploading a corrected week is safe.
          • Upgrades, curry picks and add-ons are read from the same file, so every tab updates, including Money.
          • Upload the missing weeks of Sep 4 – Oct 15 from the raw files to restore their option revenue and names.
